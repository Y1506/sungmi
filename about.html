About Us

India-Korea Joint Venture

Sungmi India Private Limited is a premier manufacturer of maritime accommodation systems. We are proud to be the first Indian manufacturer to achieve stringent Type Approvals, signifying our commitment to global maritime safety and structural standards.

With strict adherence to the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS), our products meet the baseline requirements for all international shipyard supply, as well as offshore qualifications for drilling platforms and production rigs.
